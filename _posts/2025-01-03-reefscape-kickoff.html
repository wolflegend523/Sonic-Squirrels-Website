As the calendar flips to the new year, the excitement on FRC Team 2930 is at an all-time high! After weeks of anticipation, we're officially gearing up for the 2025 FIRST Robotics Competition season—and this year, it's all about REEFSCAPE. This new game has everyone on the team buzzing with enthusiasm as we dive into the challenges and opportunities it presents. We can already tell it's going to be an exciting and action-packed season.

A New Year, A New Game

The reveal of REEFSCAPE has our team ready to hit the ground running. As with every new game, there's a mix of excitement, strategy, and some nervousness about what the challenge will be. But one thing's for sure: we're committed to pushing ourselves to the limits to build an amazing robot that can excel in every aspect of the game. REEFSCAPE introduces a fresh set of obstacles and objectives that we're eager to tackle, and we can't wait to see how our skills in engineering, programming, and teamwork will come into play this season.

Preparing for Success: Training in CAD and Programming

Over the past few months, Team 2930 has been hard at work, preparing ourselves for the upcoming season. One of the biggest focuses of our preparation has been on training—and not just any training, but targeted training in CAD (Computer-Aided Design) and programming.

CAD: Designing for Success with Onshape

Our team's CAD skills have been growing stronger year after year, and this season, we've made it a priority to build on that foundation using Onshape. Onshape has been our go-to platform for creating virtual models of our robot. It allows us to collaborate seamlessly, working on the same designs in real-time, which has been a game changer for our workflow. With the ability to make changes on the fly and get instant feedback, Onshape has given us the flexibility and tools needed to experiment with different designs for the REEFSCAPE game.

Our CAD team has been hard at work in Onshape, developing every component of our robot, from the drivetrain to the manipulators. We've put a significant amount of time into optimizing our designs for efficiency, functionality, and performance in the REEFSCAPE challenge. Plus, Onshape's cloud-based platform means that no matter where our team members are located, we can all work together without any delays.

We've also had mock design reviews, where team members critique each other's work to ensure that our designs are both practical and effective. These reviews have allowed us to improve the designs and eliminate any potential issues before they become a problem in the build season.

Programming: Ready for REEFSCAPE

On the programming side, our coders have been gearing up for the challenges REEFSCAPE will bring. With so many potential autonomous and teleoperated challenges, it's crucial that we're ready to write efficient, reliable code that will allow our robot to perform well in every aspect of the game.

Throughout the offseason, our programming team has been focused on perfecting the code for our robot's sensors, motors, and controls. They've spent countless hours experimenting with different algorithms to improve our robot's agility, responsiveness, and precision. We've also been reviewing our previous seasons' code to ensure that we can build upon our past successes and apply any lessons learned to REEFSCAPE.

From advanced pathfinding algorithms to precise control loops, the programming team has been training hard to give our robot the best chance at success on the field. Whether it's working with sensors like cameras, ultrasonic, or gyros, or developing smooth and accurate driving systems, our programmers are ready to tackle whatever challenges REEFSCAPE throws our way.

Looking Ahead

As we count down the days to the start of build season, there's a sense of excitement and anticipation on the team. We've laid the groundwork through our training in CAD and programming, and now it's time to put everything into action. We're ready to face the challenges of REEFSCAPE head-on, and we know that with hard work, creativity, and the incredible teamwork that defines FRC Team 2930, we'll be able to build a robot that's ready to perform at its best.

We want to thank everyone who has supported us so far, from our mentors to our sponsors, and we're incredibly grateful for the opportunity to compete in such an amazing program. Stay tuned for more updates as we continue to prepare for the season and, of course, we can't wait to see you all at the competitions!

Here's to an exciting 2025 FRC season—let's make it one to remember!

Go Team 2930!
